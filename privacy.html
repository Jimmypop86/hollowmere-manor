Hollowmere Manor: privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026

What the game collects

Nothing. Hollowmere Manor has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sends anything you do in the game to anyone.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device: your Remembrance, relics, pacts and unlocked investigators, your sound setting, and the night in progress so you can resume it. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it deletes all of this. It is never uploaded.

Network use

The game downloads its own files when you first open it and checks for updates when you are online. After that it plays fully offline.

Device features

Sound plays only after you turn it on in the game. The game asks your phone to vibrate briefly when you take damage, on phones that support it. It does not use your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
