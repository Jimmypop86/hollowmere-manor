How the house works
ExploreMove up to your Speed in rooms each turn. Step through an open door and the house builds a new room for you. Discovering a room ends your movement.
EndureRooms hold events, items and omens. Events test a trait: roll one die per point, each die shows 0, 1 or 2.
The hauntAfter every omen the house rolls six dice. Roll low enough and the haunt begins: a scenario chosen by the omen that woke it. If nothing wakes it, it begins at midnight (turn 18) anyway. Once it starts, monsters grow stronger every few turns.
SurviveBefore the haunt, traits can't fall below their lowest mark. After it, drop past the skull on any track and the run is over.
TogetherUp to four players share one device and pass it each turn. When the haunt begins, one of you turns traitor: you choose whom the monsters hunt, can attack the others, and chase a secret goal. Everyone else works together to stop you.
RememberEvery run, escaped or not, earns Remembrance for rooms, omens and survival. Spend it on relics and investigators. Some investigators only come after certain deeds.
